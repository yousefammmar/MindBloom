# MindBloom

Organize your studies with a beautiful, productive dashboard.

## Features
- **Dynamic Calendar**: View your schedule by day, week, or month.
- **Pomodoro Timer**: Stay focused with customizable work/break intervals.
- **Today's Stats**: Track your daily events, completion rate, and focus time.
- **Quick Tasks**: Manage small to-dos on the fly.
- **Multiple Themes**: Customize your experience with beautiful dashboard styles.
